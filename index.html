Privacy Policy

Orbit & Empire Uploader and the Orbit & Empire YouTube channel (@Orbit_Empire)
Last updated: 2026-11-09

This policy explains what data the Orbit & Empire Uploader ("the app") accesses, how it is used, and where it is stored. The app is a private tool operated by the channel owner. It is not offered to other users.

1. Who we are

Operator: Tapajyoti Deb
Contact: tapajyotideb@gmail.com

2. Data the app accesses

When the operator signs in with Google, the app requests these YouTube API scopes:

  • youtube.upload: upload videos to the operator's own YouTube channel.
  • youtube: manage the operator's own channel, for example set video metadata, thumbnails, captions and playlists.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: read the operator's own channel analytics (views, watch time, retention, click-through rate).

The app only reads and writes data for the channel that signed in. It does not access data about other YouTube users, their comments, subscriptions or personal information.

3. How the data is used

  • Uploading and scheduling the channel's own videos.
  • Setting titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions and playlists on those videos.
  • Pulling channel analytics into local reports so the operator can review performance.

YouTube API data is used only for these purposes. It is not sold, rented, shared for advertising, or used to build profiles of individuals.

4. Storage and security

  • The OAuth client secret and the access and refresh tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer, in a folder that is excluded from version control.
  • Analytics reports are stored locally on that computer.
  • The app does not run a server and does not send YouTube data to any third party.

5. Third parties

The app communicates only with Google's APIs (googleapis.com) to read and write the operator's channel. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app uses no advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens and local reports stay on the operator's computer until deleted by the operator. To stop access, revoke the app at Google Account permissions, then delete the local token file. Uploaded videos and channel data remain on YouTube until removed through YouTube Studio.

7. Your choices

Access to the channel can be revoked at any time through Google Account permissions. For questions or deletion requests, contact the address in section 1.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version. Material changes will be noted on this page.
